Features

Everything the tool does, in plain terms.

Accounts and routing

Group accounts by topic

Each destination account is given one or more categories — for example books or films. A video tagged books is offered to every account that accepts books, on whichever platform. One account can be a catch-all that receives everything.

Several accounts per platform

You can connect more than one account on the same platform and keep their topics, schedules and timezones completely separate.

Enable and disable without losing anything

Turning an account off stops it receiving new posts. Its settings and history stay intact, so you can turn it back on later.

Scheduling

Fixed times or spaced intervals

Give an account a list of clock times — 19:00 and 21:00, say — or ask for a number of posts per day spaced a set number of hours apart.

Per-account timezone

Times are interpreted in the timezone you set on each account, so an account aimed at a different region posts at the right local hour.

A calendar you can act on

Every queued post appears on a day-by-day calendar with its time, its destination account, its caption and its thumbnail. From the same row you can publish it immediately, skip it, or remove it.

Minimum gap between posts

Set a minimum spacing so two posts to the same account never land back to back.

Catch-up after downtime

If the computer was switched off when a post was due, that post goes out as soon as the tool is running again rather than being lost.

Captions

Edit before anything is published

Every caption is editable in the interface. Changes are saved to the queued post, so an edit made after scheduling is still respected at posting time.

Keep the original wording

One click restores the caption exactly as it was written, if you prefer it to any rewrite.

Consistent style

Choose a tone, a language, a length target, how many hashtags to end with, and whether to include emoji or a call to action. You can also add your own instructions in your own words.

Credit the original creator

Optionally append or prepend a credit line naming the account a video came from. It is never added twice.

A fixed prefix or suffix

Add a standing line — a series name, a disclaimer — to the start or end of every caption.

Media

Videos

Standard short-form video posts, published in the destination platform's native format.

Photo carousels

A set of images is published as a single carousel post where the platform supports it, keeping the images in their original order.

Thumbnails

A preview frame is kept for every post so you can recognise it at a glance in the calendar and in the results list.

Control and safety

Manual approval mode

An account can be left on manual approval: its posts wait in the calendar until you press publish. Automatic posting is opt-in, per account.

No double posting

A post that is already being uploaded, or that has already gone out, cannot be published a second time — whether the second attempt comes from another browser tab or from the scheduler itself.

Retries with backoff

A failed attempt is retried a few times with increasing delays, then marked as failed with the reason kept.

PIN lock

The interface can be locked with a PIN, which is required if you make it reachable from outside your own computer.

Posting log

Every attempt is recorded with its outcome. Accounts that need to be reconnected raise a visible alert.

Exporting

Hand-off for manual posting

The whole plan can be exported as ordinary folders — one per destination account, each holding the videos in posting order, a caption file beside each one, a cover image and a summary of the schedule. Useful when you would rather upload something by hand.
