How it works

From connecting an account to seeing a post go out, and what each permission is actually used for.

1. Install and open it

Reels Poster runs on your own computer. Starting it opens a dashboard in your browser at a local address. Nothing is uploaded anywhere at this point — the tool is simply a program running on your machine.

2. Connect an account

For each account you want to post to, you press Connect and are taken to that platform's own login and consent screen. You sign in as yourself and decide whether to grant access. The tool never sees your password.

What comes back is an access token tied to that one account. It is stored on your computer and used only to publish the posts you schedule.

You can withdraw access at any time from the platform's own settings — for example under connected or authorised apps. The tool immediately stops being able to post to that account.

3. Prepare your posts

Add your videos to the tool. Each post gets a caption, which you can edit freely, and a category such as books or films. The category is what decides which accounts it will be offered to.

4. Set the schedule

Each destination account has its own posting times and its own timezone. Once set, queued posts are given slots automatically and appear on the calendar, so you can see the week at a glance and rearrange it before anything is published.

5. Publish

At the scheduled time the tool uploads the post through the platform's official API. If the account is set to manual approval, nothing goes out until you press publish on that row yourself.

Each attempt is written to a log with its outcome. A failure is retried a few times with increasing delays, and if it still fails the reason is kept so you can see what happened.

What each permission is used for

PermissionWhy it is needed
Basic profile information To show which account you connected, so several connected accounts can be told apart in the interface. No profile data is stored beyond the display name shown on the account card.
Upload content to the account To send a prepared video to the account so its owner can finish and publish it from the platform's own app.
Publish content to the account To publish a post at the scheduled time, with the caption and privacy setting chosen in the tool beforehand.

Only the permissions needed for the way you have configured an account are requested. An account set to hand videos over for manual finishing is never asked for publishing rights.

What the tool does not do

  • It does not post to any account that has not been connected by its own owner.
  • It does not read, collect or store other people's private information.
  • It does not follow, like, comment, or message anyone.
  • It does not share your access tokens with anyone — they stay on your computer.

If something goes wrong

Common cases and what they mean are listed in the FAQ. If an account's access has expired or been withdrawn, the tool stops posting to it and shows an alert asking you to connect it again.
