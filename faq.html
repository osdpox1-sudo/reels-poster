Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the things people ask most.

Using it

Do I need to create an account on this website?

No. There is no sign-up and no login here. This site is documentation. The tool itself runs on your own computer, and the only accounts involved are your own social accounts, which you connect from inside the app.

Can I use it for accounts I do not own?

No. An account can only be connected by signing in as its owner and granting access on the platform's own consent screen. The tool cannot post anywhere that has not been authorised that way.

Does it need my password?

Never. Connecting an account happens on the platform's own login page. The tool receives an access token afterwards and never sees your password.

Does my computer have to be on?

Yes. The scheduler runs inside the app on your machine, so it has to be running when a post is due. If it was off, the post goes out as soon as you start it again.

Can I change my mind after scheduling something?

Yes. Until a post has gone out you can edit its caption, move it, skip it or remove it. Edits made after scheduling are applied to the queued post.

Data and access

Where are my videos and tokens stored?

On your own computer, in the app's folder. Nothing is uploaded to this website, and there is no central service holding your content. See the Privacy Policy for the detail.

How do I disconnect an account?

Two ways, and either is enough. Remove the account inside the app, which deletes its stored token; or revoke access from the platform's own settings under connected or authorised apps.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app's folder from your computer. That removes the videos, captions, schedules, logs and tokens, because that is the only place they exist. Steps are on the Support page.

Posting

Why is my post waiting instead of publishing?

Most likely the account is set to manual approval, which means posts wait for you to publish them. It can also be that no connected account matches that post's category, or that the scheduled time has not arrived yet.

Why did a video arrive without its caption?

Some platforms accept a caption only when a post is published directly. If an account is configured to hand the video over for you to finish in the platform's own app, the caption cannot travel with it — the app shows the caption so you can copy it across in one tap.

Is there a limit to how much I can post?

Yes, and it is set by each platform, not by this tool. The app shows how much of the daily allowance an account has used, and stops rather than pushing past a limit.

A post failed. What happens?

It is retried a few times with increasing delays. If it still fails it is marked as failed and the reason is kept in the log, so nothing disappears silently.

Can it post the same video twice by accident?

No. A post that is already uploading, or that has already gone out, cannot be published again — including from a second browser tab or from the scheduler at the same moment.
