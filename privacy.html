Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 August 2026

Summary

Reels Poster runs on the user's own computer. It has no servers, no user accounts and no analytics. Everything it handles — videos, captions, schedules and access tokens — is stored locally on that computer and is never transmitted to us, because there is no "us" to transmit it to.

Who this applies to

This policy covers people who install and run Reels Poster, and visitors to this website.

What the app handles

DataWhyWhere it is kept
Access tokens for the accounts you connect To publish the posts you schedule to those accounts On your computer only
Basic profile information of connected accounts (display name, avatar) To show which account is connected, so several can be told apart On your computer only
Videos, images, captions and schedules you add To publish them at the times you choose On your computer only
A log of posting attempts and their outcomes So you can see what was posted and what failed On your computer only

What we never collect

  • Passwords. Connecting an account happens on the platform's own login page; the app never sees your credentials.
  • Other people's private data. The app does not read direct messages, follower lists, or any private information belonging to anyone.
  • Analytics, tracking or advertising identifiers. This website sets no cookies and runs no trackers.

Sharing

Your data is not sold, rented or shared. The app communicates with exactly two kinds of destination:

  • The platforms you connect — to publish your posts, using their official APIs.
  • Services you configure yourself — if you choose to enable an optional third-party service inside the app, it is used only for the purpose you enabled it for, with the credentials you supply.

Some platforms publish a video by downloading it from a web address rather than receiving the file directly. When that is required, the app makes the specific file reachable at a temporary, unguessable address only for as long as the upload takes, then withdraws it. The setting controlling this is visible and can be pointed at a location you control.

Retention

Files stay on your computer until you delete them. The app does not keep copies anywhere else, and nothing expires on our side because there is no server side.

Your choices

  • Withdraw access at any time — from the platform's own settings, or by removing the account inside the app.
  • Delete everything — deleting the app's folder removes all stored data. Full steps are on the Support page.
  • Choose what is connected — no account is involved unless you connect it yourself.

Children

The app is not directed at children and should only be used by people old enough to hold accounts on the platforms they connect.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page is updated. Changes that affect what data is handled will be described here.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: see the Support page for the contact address.
