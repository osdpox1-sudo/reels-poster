Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 July 2026

Reels Poster ("the app") is a personal, self-hosted scheduling tool operated by a single creator for their own TikTok accounts. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

1. Who operates this app

The app is operated privately by its owner and is not offered as a public service. It runs on the operator's own computer and is used only with the operator's own TikTok accounts.

2. What data the app handles

  • TikTok access and refresh tokens — issued after the operator authorises their own TikTok account. They are used solely to upload videos to that account.
  • Basic account information — the account nickname and identifier returned by TikTok, used to show which account a video will be posted to.
  • Video files and captions — the operator's own content, stored as ordinary files on the operator's computer.
  • Scheduling and log records — posting times, statuses and error messages, kept locally so the operator can see what was posted and when.

3. Where data is stored

All data is stored locally on the operator's own computer in plain files. The app has no server, no hosted database, and no user accounts. Nothing is uploaded anywhere except the videos and captions the operator chooses to publish, which go directly to TikTok through TikTok's official API.

4. Data sharing

No data is sold, rented, or shared with any third party. The only external service the app communicates with is TikTok's own API, for the purpose of publishing the operator's content.

5. Data retention and deletion

Data stays on the operator's computer until deleted. Tokens can be removed at any time by deleting them in the app, and access can be revoked at any time from TikTok's own settings under "Manage app permissions". Revoking access immediately stops the app from being able to post.

6. Other people's data

The app does not collect information about other TikTok users. It does not read followers, messages, comments, or analytics about anyone else.

7. Security

Access tokens are stored on the operator's own machine, protected by that computer's own account security. The app is not exposed to the public internet by default.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children and is not made available to anyone other than its operator.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "last updated" date.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the contact email listed for this app in the TikTok developer portal.
