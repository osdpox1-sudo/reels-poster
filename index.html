Plan a week of posts in one sitting.

Reels Poster is a self-hosted scheduling tool for a single creator. It keeps your short-form videos, their captions and their posting times in one place, then publishes them to your own TikTok, Instagram and Threads accounts at the times you choose.

What it is

Posting consistently across three platforms means repeating the same chore every day: find the file, retype the caption, pick a time, do it again on the next account. Reels Poster removes the repetition. You prepare your videos once, decide which account and which time each one belongs to, and the tool handles the publishing.

It runs on your own computer. There is no account to create here, no subscription, and no server holding your videos — the app talks directly to the official APIs of the platforms you connect.

What it does for you

One queue, several accounts

Group your accounts by topic — books, films, and so on — and each video goes to the accounts that match its category.

Times you control

Set the posting times per account, in that account's own timezone. A calendar shows exactly what goes out and when.

Captions you can edit

Write a caption, adjust it, or restore the original wording. Nothing is published until you are happy with it.

Videos and photo carousels

Post a single video, or a set of images as one carousel, depending on what the destination platform supports.

Approve or automate

Leave an account on manual approval and confirm each post yourself, or let it publish on schedule once you trust it.

A clear record

Every attempt is logged with its result, so you can always see what was posted, what failed, and why.

How it works, briefly

  1. Connect your accounts You authorise each of your own accounts once, through the platform's official login screen. Access can be withdrawn at any time from the platform's own settings.
  2. Prepare your posts Add videos, review the captions, and assign each one a category.
  3. Set the schedule Choose posting times per account. The calendar fills in automatically.
  4. Publish Posts go out at their scheduled time, or wait for your approval — whichever you chose for that account.

Where it can post

PlatformWhat is published
TikTok Videos, using the official Content Posting API. Depending on the permissions granted, a video is either sent to the account's inbox for the owner to finish, or published directly.
Instagram Reels and image carousels, using the official Instagram Platform content publishing API.
Threads Video posts and image carousels, using the official Threads API.

Only your own accounts. Reels Poster publishes to accounts whose owner has personally authorised it. It does not read other people's private data and never posts anywhere the account holder has not connected themselves.

Who it is for

A single creator running a handful of their own accounts, who would rather spend ten minutes once a week than a few minutes every day. It is not a marketing suite, not a team product, and there is nothing to sign up for.
