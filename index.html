Reels Poster

A personal content scheduling tool for TikTok.

What this app does

Reels Poster is a small self-hosted tool used by a single creator to organise their own short-form videos and publish them to their own TikTok account on a schedule. It runs locally on the creator's computer — there is no public website, no sign-up, and no multi-user service.

How it works

  • The creator adds video files and captions to a local queue.
  • Each video is assigned to one of the creator's own TikTok accounts based on its content category (for example books, films or games).
  • The tool schedules posting times in the creator's chosen timezone and uploads each video through TikTok's official Content Posting API.
  • The creator authorises their own TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit; access can be revoked at any time from TikTok's settings.
