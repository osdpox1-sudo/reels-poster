Disconnecting an account

Either of these is enough, and both take effect immediately.

From inside the app

Open the app, find the account, and remove it. Its stored access token is deleted with it and the app can no longer post to that account.

From the platform

  • TikTok — Profile → Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions, then remove the app.
  • Instagram — Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites, then remove the app.
  • Threads — Settings → Account → Website permissions, then remove the app.

Deleting your data

Because the app runs on your own computer and stores everything there, deleting your data means deleting those files. There is no server-side copy to request removal of.

  1. Close the app So nothing is writing while you delete.
  2. Delete the app's folder This removes the downloaded videos, thumbnails, captions, schedules, posting logs and stored access tokens.
  3. Revoke access on each platform Using the steps above, so no token remains valid even in a backup.

Posts that were already published stay on your social accounts — they belong to your account, not to this app. Delete them from the platform itself if you want them removed.

Reporting a problem

If a post fails, the app records the reason in its posting log. Including that message when you get in touch makes it much quicker to work out what happened.
